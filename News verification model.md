## **Project overview**

> Title: Natural Language Processing and Conversational AI for Fake News Detection  
> **Type:** Independent research and development project  
> **Start date:** 24 September 2026  
> **Status:** Ongoing (initial research and design phase)
> 
> **Background and motivation:** misinformation spreads quickly on digital media and can cause social, political, and personal harm. Manual fact-checking is slow and cannot keep pace with the volume of content, which motivates automated, accessible verification tools.
> 
> **Problem statement:** users lack an accessible way to check whether a news item comes from a credible source and to see how other outlets report the same event.
> 
>objectives :

> 1. Assess news credibility using source reliability and author verification.
> 2. Retrieve related news to provide corroborating or contrasting context.
> 3. Provide a conversational interface so users can query and verify news in natural language.
> 4. Support concurrent use by multiple users (minimum target: two simultaneous users) without interference between sessions.
> 5. Review existing fake news detection methods and adopt additional techniques where they address limitations of the initial design.

**Add a short non-functional requirements section:**

> **Non-functional requirements:** the system shall handle at least two concurrent users, keeping each user's session and results separate. Scalability beyond this baseline is out of scope for the initial phase.
> **Scope and methodology:** the initial phase covers a literature review of fake news detection approaches (content-based, source-based, and hybrid), followed by prototype development. The tools, datasets, and evaluation metrics will be finalised after this review and documented here.
> 
> **Development approach:** iterative. Features are added as gaps are identified, with design decisions and changes logged in this documentation.
> 
> **Tools:** GitHub (version control), Obsidian (documentation). The technical stack is to be determined.
